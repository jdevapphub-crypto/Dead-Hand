DEAD HAND
by Joheliv Labs

DEAD HAND is a personal safety app that notifies your trusted contacts through push notifications if you don't check in — and lets you locate a loved one with their unique code.

🛡️ Safety Mode

Set a check-in timer. If you don't check in, your partners get a push notification with your last known location.

👥 Group Safety Mode

Invite 2–4 friends to a shared group using a code. Everyone sees each other live on a map.

🔍 Find My Device

Enter a person's unique code to see their last known location and online status.

📜 PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 2026

Joheliv Labs ("we", "us") respects your privacy.

1. What We Collect

  • GPS location — to include a map link in alerts and show your last known position when someone uses your code.
  • Email & password — to sign you in via Firebase Authentication.
  • Display name and unique code — so others can locate you or receive your alerts.

2. Who Sees Your Data

  • People you share your code with — can see your name, online status, and last known location.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.
